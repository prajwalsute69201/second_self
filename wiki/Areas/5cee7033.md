Batting is an important part of cricket. The batsman tries to score runs by hitting the ball with the bat. Runs can be scored by running between the wickets or by hitting boundaries. A boundary that reaches the edge of the field after bouncing is worth four runs, while a ball hit over the boundary without bouncing is worth six.
